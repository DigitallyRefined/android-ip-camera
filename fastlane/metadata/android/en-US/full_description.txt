<h3>Features</h3><ul><li>🌎 Built in server, just open the video stream in a web browser, video app or even set it as a camera for <a href="https://github.com/blakeblackshear/frigate">Frigate</a> or a Home Assistant MJPEG IP Camera (using `https://[ip_address]:4444/video/mjpeg`)</li><li>📴 Option to turn the display off while streaming</li><li>📡 Optional RTSP stream (H.264 video + AAC audio) for mpv, Frigate/go2rtc, NVRs and other RTSP clients (using `rtsps://[ip_address]:8554/live`, plus a lighter sub stream at `/live/sub`)</li><li>🛰️ Optional ONVIF camera mode with WS-Discovery, snapshots and motion events for UniFi Protect, Home Assistant and other NVRs</li><li>🤳 Switch between the main or selfie camera</li><li>🏃‍➡️ Motion detection & notifications (via ONVIF), automatic recording on motion & continuous recording (with automatic deletion of old recordings)</li><li>🎛️ Remote web interface with controls for camera section, image rotation, manual video recording, audio/video sync, flash light toggle, resolution, zoom, exposure, contrast and optional GPU noise reduction</li><li>🌙 Night-sight mode: on phones with a astrophotography/night-sight photo mode, camera still photos are continually taken & are streamed as video. An optional automatic mode can follow sunrise/sunset (−6° civil twilight), the phone's ambient light sensor, or either.</li><li>📝 Optional burned-in text overlay (time, date, camera name, battery, Wi-Fi and battery temperature) for H.264/MJPEG streams, RTSP and recordings, plus an optional overheat-pause threshold.</li><li>✂️ Crop the live stream (and snapshots) to a rectangle drawn over the preview; the crop is applied on the phone</li><li>☁️ Optionally connect your device to a hosted or <a href="https://github.com/DigitallyRefined/android-ip-camera/tree/main/opentunnel-server">self-hosted</a> cloud <a href="https://github.com/anomalyco/opentunnel">OpenTunnel</a> server for remote access when you're not on your home/work network</li><li>🖼️ Choose between different image quality settings and frame rates (to help reduce phone over heating)</li><li>🛂 Username and password protection</li><li>🔐 Automatic TLS certificate support to protect stream and login details via HTTPS/RTSPS</li><li>🥾 Optional start on boot</li></ul><h3>Warning</h3>

<p>If you are planning to run this 24/7, please make sure that your phone does not stay at 100% charge. Doing so may damage the battery and cause it to swell up, which could cause it to explode.</p>

<p>Some models include an option to only charge to 80%, make sure this is enabled where possible.</p>

<p>Note: running at a higher image quality may cause some phones to over heat, which can also damage the battery.</p>

<p>Continuous streaming - whether from an always-on camera mode (**Camera always on**, **Motion clips** or **Continuous recording**), a browser or an NVR - can cause some devices to over heat and power off. Use **Pause streaming at battery temperature** under **Stream → Device Safety** to protect the device.</p>
